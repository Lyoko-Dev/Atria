# Atria

## A space for your system

Atria is a private app for plural systems. It brings together tools to help
you organize everyday life, get to know each identity better, and support your
well-being.

## What can you do with Atria?

- Create profiles with names, pronouns, colours, images, and personal details.
- Record who is fronting and who is co-fronting.
- Write a private journal and save personal notes.
- Track feelings and observe changes over time.
- Organize appointments, routines, reminders, projects, and tasks.
- Save wishes, decisions, boundaries, and internal relationships.
- Keep track of books, films, games, and other interests.
- Manage expenses, budgets, savings, and recurring payments.
- Use private spaces for internal communication and Headspace.
- Connect devices, add friends, and chat when online features are enabled.

## Your privacy comes first

You can use Atria without creating an account. Your information is stored on
your device, and online features are optional.

If you enable them, you choose what to share. Private data is protected before
it leaves your device, and online backups are encrypted.

## Get started

Open Atria at:

<https://atria.lyokodev.com>

You can install it from your browser's sharing or options menu.

Atria is available in Spanish and English. You can change the language in the
app settings.
