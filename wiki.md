# Atria guide

This is the current Atria guide. It is organized around everyday use, not by
version.

## Getting started

You can use Atria without creating an account. Your information is stored on
your device, and online features are optional.

## System profiles

Create a profile for each identity and customize its name, pronouns, colour,
image, role, and other details. You can also save private notes, internal
relationships, boundaries, and other useful information.

## Fronting

Record who is fronting and when co-fronting happens. Atria keeps a history that
you can review later.

## Journal, notes, and feelings

Write private journal entries, save notes, record feelings, and look back at
changes over time.

## Calendar and routines

Use the calendar for events, appointments, routines, tasks, and reminders.

## Projects, finances, and library

Organize projects and outstanding tasks. Track expenses, budgets, savings,
recurring payments, books, films, games, and other interests.

## Headspace and internal communication

Atria includes private spaces for internal communication and Headspace
organization. Their content stays on the device unless you choose to share it.

## Online features and backups

When enabled, online features let you create an account, connect devices, add
friends, use chat, synchronize compatible information, and create or restore
encrypted online backups. You choose what to share.

You can also export a manual backup and import it later.

## Upgrading from Atria 0.12 to 0.13

Local data from Atria 0.12 is kept when upgrading to Atria 0.13. The app
recognizes the previous format and adds new options when appropriate.

Migration does not require manual action. As a precaution, create a backup
before upgrading.

## Privacy and help

Atria is designed to work locally, and its main features do not require an
account. If something does not work as expected, make sure the app is updated
and that you have a recent backup before reporting the issue.
