Español

Getting started

Start using Atria without an account, change language, or install the app from your browser.

Alters and profiles

Create profiles with names, pronouns, images, roles, and personal details. Manage their notes and relationships.

Fronting

Record who is fronting and who is co-fronting. Review your history and statistics.

Journal, notes, and feelings

Write private entries, save notes, and record emotional states for later review.

Calendar and routines

Organize events, appointments, reminders, routines, and tasks.

Projects, finances, and library

Manage projects, expenses, budgets, savings, recurring payments, books, films, games, and interests.

Headspace and relationships

Use private spaces for internal communication, Headspace, and system relationships.

Polls and analysis

Create internal polls and review activity and emotional analysis. Analysis describes your records; it does not diagnose or predict.

Online features

Create an account, connect devices, add friends, use chat, and control their permissions.

Sync and backups

Synchronize compatible information and create encrypted backups. Manual export and import are also available.

Privacy

Atria works locally and online features are optional. Private data is protected before it leaves your device.

From 0.12 to 0.13

The upgrade keeps your local data and recognizes the previous format. Migration is automatic; a backup is recommended first.
